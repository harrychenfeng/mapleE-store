The ME Pro 680 is Maple Electronics' flagship brushless hedge trimmer, built for professionals and homeowners who demand high efficiency, low noise, and long runtime. Brushless motor technology eliminates the heat, noise, and frequent maintenance of traditional motors.

The 65cm dual-blade bar covers a wider area in each pass, dramatically boosting efficiency. High-carbon steel blades are precision-ground for smooth, clean cuts through branches up to 22mm thick. The ergonomic grip reduces hand fatigue during extended sessions.

Powered by the Maple Electronics universal 20V battery platform — compatible with 80+ tools in the lineup. A 45-minute charge delivers 90–120 minutes of full-load runtime.

Is the battery compatible with other Maple Electronics tools?+

The ME Pro 680 uses the Maple Electronics universal 20V platform, fully compatible with 80+ tools in the lineup (mowers, blowers, chainsaws, etc.). The 40V kit version is compatible with the 40V platform series.

Can I use it in the rain?+

The ME Pro 680 is rated IP54, protecting against light rain and water splashes. Light drizzle is fine. We don't recommend use in heavy rain or standing water, as this may affect longevity and safety.
